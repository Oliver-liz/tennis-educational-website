Tennis Rules and Scoring

How the Game Works

How Tennis is Played

A tennis match starts with a serve. The player serving tries to hit the ball into the correct service box. After the serve, the players hit the ball back and forth until one player makes a mistake.

Players need to keep the ball inside the court and return it before it bounces twice. Tennis requires players to move quickly and think about where they want to hit the ball.

Tennis Scoring

Tennis uses a scoring system of love, 15, 30, and 40. A player normally needs to win four points with a two-point advantage to win a game.

Basic Steps of a Tennis Point

  1. The player serves the ball.
  2. The opponent returns the ball.
  3. The players continue the rally.
  4. The point ends when a player makes a mistake.

Basic Rules

Players must serve from behind the baseline and the serve must land in the correct service box.

A player wins a point when the opponent hits the ball out of the court, does not return it, or makes another mistake.
