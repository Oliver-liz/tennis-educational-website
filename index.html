Tennis: History and Basics

Learn About the Sport of Tennis

What is Tennis?

Tennis is a sport that can be played between two players or two teams of two players. Players use a racket to hit a ball over a net and try to make it difficult for the opponent to return the ball.

Tennis can be played on hard courts, clay courts, and grass courts. The sport requires speed, coordination, movement, and strategy.

History of Tennis

Tennis developed from older games played in Europe. Over time, the sport became more organized and modern tennis developed with official rules and competitions.

Modern Tennis

Today, tennis is played around the world by people of different ages and skill levels. Professional tennis also has many important tournaments.
